03Pacing

We want a delay between messages to match the speed at which humans digest information. Sending three messages in the same second overloads the lead’s absorption and makes the conversation feel overwhelming.

Principle

One beat at a time

After the lead writes, the bot takes a short thinking pause (about 1–2 seconds). Each following message waits for a reading gap based on the length of the one before it (about 1.2 to 4 seconds). Quick-reply buttons appear only on the last message of a turn.

05Branches and outcomes

Every path ends with a clear next step. Unqualified leads are not left hanging — they get a short close and the masterclass.

Human

07Out of scope / Later

Deferred past v1 — not in the first launch.

PDF guide lead magnet

v1 sends no guide. Later: mention it in the welcome and send the PDF to every lead, qualified or not.

Dedup and out-of-order handling

v1 processes events as they arrive. A duplicate can repeat a step; an out-of-order reply can hit the wrong question. Later: dedupe and a per-contact queue.

Charts and images in DMs

On hold. The conversation is text and quick-reply buttons only.
