# Pollinate — vibrate the trellis wires

Daily wire-tap to shake pollen loose.

Why → [`science/fruit.md`](../../science/fruit.md)


## When

- **Before lunch** — driest part of the day.
- **Every weekday.**

Flower viability is 1 to 2 days. Miss 3 days and you fall short
of your fruit-load target.


## Who

- Delegate. Not an owner job.


## How

1. Tie tie-wraps on the wire as tapping points.
2. Tape the wire in the middle, between the two wires.
3. Tap or twang the **wires**, not the plants.
4. Start a quarter of the way in → watch where the wire shakes →
   move along to reach the rest.
5. Both beds at once.


## Experimental — bigger operations

Bumblebee hives. One hive covers ~1,000 to 15,000 sq ft, lasts
~7 to 9 weeks. Worth it for:

- Screened houses.
- Houses wider than ~30 ft.
- Very early / late season, no active pollinators.
- Urban sites with no wild bees.

Electric flower vibrators — not recommended.
