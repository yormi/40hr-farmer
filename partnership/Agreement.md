THE 40-HOUR FARMER PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

This agreement is between Orisha ("we") and the partner grower signing below ("you").

1. PURPOSE
We team up to bring industrial yields to your greenhouse tomato beds. Orisha provides the climate control gear, step-by-step growing instructions and support. You grow and sell the tomatoes.
Target: an additional $5,000 to $12,000 in yields per bed over the season. This is a target, not a promise. The refund guarantee below is what we commit to.

2. ORISHA'S RESPONSIBILITIES
- Help for one greenhouse.
- Climate control and irrigation automation gear for that greenhouse.
- A structure to plan the season with you
- Step-by-step instructions from variety choice to crop-out.
- Weekly crop-steering analysis based on your Monday check-ins.
- Technical help by phone, Monday to Friday, 9 am to 4 pm ET.
- Growing questions answered by email by Antoine within the week.
- Proactive follow-up: Antoine reaches out when your numbers show something is off.

3. YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES
- Install the automation equipement before February
- Tend the beds: 3 to 4 hours per bed per week.
- Sell the tomatoes. Our fees are capped. All extra sales revenue belong to you.
- Fill in the Monday check-in (about 2 minutes) to log crop measurements.
- Speak up when something does not fit your farm.
- Pay for propane, heating, dehumidification and bed labor.

4. REQUIREMENTS
You confirm that, by January 31, 2027, the farm will have:
- A greenhouse built to carry a 2-wire lower-and-lean trellis.
- A heater with enough output to keep temperature above 65'F on spring nights.
- Frost-proof drip irrigation with constant pressure.
- A budget set aside for propane covering both heating and dehumidification.
- Indeterminate slicer, heirloom, or cherry varieties, transplanted between February 1 and April 30, 2027.

5. FEES
- $350 USD per month
- No minimum commitment.
- Either party may end the partnership at any time with written notice (email is enough).
- The refund guarantee in section 6 applies to fees already paid.

6. REFUND GUARANTEE
If the increase in your tomato yields does not cover the fees paid to Orisha, Orisha refunds the fees paid for the season.
- To qualify, you must fill in the Monday check-ins during the season.
- You judge the yield increase. We trust you to be fair with us.
- Propane and labor costs are borne by you and are not refunded.
- Outside of the refund guarantee, Orisha's liability is limited to the fees paid under this agreement.

7. EQUIPMENT
Orisha supplies the equipment for the duration of the partnership. When the partnership ends, you will have to send the equipement at your own expense.
