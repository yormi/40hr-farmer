# Thin fruit — you set the load, not the plant

Greenhouse tomatoes. Prune clusters down to a fixed fruit count
so the plant isn't overloaded. Fruit is the **single biggest
energy drain** — too many exhausts the plant and thins the stem
(a mature fruit seen on a stem thinner than a BIC pen).


## Target — summer

- **No more than 3 fruit per cluster, on all clusters.**
- **Consistency matters most.** A bed of 1-fruit then 7-fruit
  clusters wrecks uniform irrigation / climate and steady sales.
- Cheating extra fruit while load is low → never above **4** per
  cluster.


## When

- Start thinning to 3 fruit/cluster **~8 weeks before**
  heat-wave season.
- Prune **weekly** — early is enough.


## How

- Prune fruit **as early as possible** — before it reaches full
  size, when it drains the most sugar.
- **Do not** prune full-size fruit → the sugar's already spent.
- On big clusters, favor pruning the **smaller** fruit.
- Prune **evenly across clusters** → consistent harvest →
  consistent load for vigor management.
- Also cut the **tiny laggard fruit** far behind their
  clustermates → never sellable, just burns sugar.


## Worth it?

Heartbreaking to cut what you sell, but everything else grows
faster, remaining fruit sizes up bigger, and the plant stays
resilient.
