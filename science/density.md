# Density — stems per m² from light


## Model


### Light to stems


- **outside DLI** (monthly, by region) × **house transmission** → inside DLI   (double poly ≈ 0.6–0.65)

- **inside DLI at full fruit load** → density target

- density (stems/m²) = inside DLI at full load ÷ 7, clamped to **2.0–4.0**


### Fruit load


- **fruit at full load** → ~60–70% of assimilates   (fruit is the dominant sink)

- **transplant, 1 truss** → little fruit demand → ~5 mol per stem enough early on

- **full load** → ~7–9 mol per stem needed   (shortfall → flower abortion, small fruit, thin heads)

- **February transplant** → full load lands ~8–10 weeks later, in brighter months → density sized to that light, above the February-only number

- **crop carried at full load through low light** → stems alone can't balance it   (below 2.0 floor wastes light)

- **truss pruning** → balances demand to light while keeping a full canopy   (3–4 fruit per truss in dark weeks)


### Fruit size


- **beefsteaks** (e.g. Contessa, Kardinata) → ~8–9 mol per stem instead of 7 → ~10–15% fewer stems   (big fruit needs more light per stem)


### Heat


- **hot summers** → lower density   (better air movement, less plant stress)


### Rising light


- **extra side shoot** on every 2nd–4th plant → added stems as light rises   (starting ~4–6 weeks after planting)


## Vocabulary


- **DLI** — daily light integral, mol/m²/day of photosynthetic light.

- **full fruit load** — every truss on the plant carrying fruit, from first set to harvest.
