## Speaker convention (Month 2+)

A video can be carried by **one person or two** — pick by who actually
knows the topic, not by a fixed format. Keep the same three beats either
way.

- **Whoever knows the beat best leads it.** Assign each beat to the
  person with the most real authority on that specific point (Andrew for
  book/breadth agronomy, Antoine for lean/practitioner moves), so the
  person talking is always the one who knows it cold.
- **Solo when one person owns the whole topic.** If a single expert knows
  every beat best, the video is theirs alone. Don't add a second speaker
  for format's sake.
- **Two when the beats split.** Pair up only when different beats have
  different best-knowers. Tag each beat with `[Speaker leads]` and note
  who reacts.
- Pairings/solos are topic-driven: Antoine alone, Andrew alone, Guillaume
  + Antoine, Guillaume + Andrew, or Antoine + Andrew. Guillaume need not
  be on camera.

Example beat:

```
1. **<Point>** [Andrew leads, Antoine reacts]
   - Concept: ...
   - Visible signal: ...
   - Number: ...
```
