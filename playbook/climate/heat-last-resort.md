# Heat — the last resort

What to do when the house is still too hot after everything
else.

Why → [`science/climate.md`](../../science/climate.md)


## Precondition

Reach for this only after irrigation, pruning, fruit load,
feeding, and HAF are all tuned.


## Lever 0 — water supply

- Transpiration is the cooling. If irrigation can't supply it,
  you lose all of it.
- → [`irrigation/irrigation-baseline-plan.md`](../irrigation/irrigation-baseline-plan.md)


## Lever 1 — reduce fruit load

~2 °F of tolerance per fruit dropped.

| Situation | Do |
|---|---|
| Sales pause coming | prune a whole cluster **~7 weeks** ahead |
| Otherwise | new clusters to **2.5 fruit** (alternate 3, 2) |
| Vigor still not recovering | **2 fruit** |
| Quick fix | pull from older clusters not yet full-size |

→ [`fruit-load/fruit-thinning.md`](../fruit-load/fruit-thinning.md)


## Lever 2 — shade, last

- 1 % light lost = 1 % yield lost, every day it's deployed.
- Only helps when roots genuinely can't supply transpiration
  demand.


## Experimental

Not locked protocol. Confirm before running.

- **30 % diffusing shade** — theory ~5 °F under July sun; real
  win is cutting water demand.
- **Night window AC** — close sides, 25,000 BTU at night.
  Theory ~5 °F, two units ~10 °F, ~$1/night per °F. Watch
  humidity. Untested.
