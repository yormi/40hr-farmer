# Density — stems per m² from light


## Model


### Light to stems


- **outside DLI** (monthly, by region) × **house transmission** → inside DLI   (double poly ≈ 0.6–0.65)

- **inside DLI averaged over the fruit-setting window** → density target   (window: planting → ~8 weeks after)

- density (stems/m²) = average inside DLI ÷ 7, clamped to **2.0–4.0**


### Fruit size


- **beefsteaks** (e.g. Contessa, Kardinata) → ~8–9 mol per stem instead of 7 → ~10–15% fewer stems   (big fruit needs more light per stem)


### Heat


- **hot summers** → lower density   (better air movement, less plant stress)


### Rising light


- density set at planting matches the low-light window

- **extra side shoot** on every 2nd–4th plant → added stems as light rises   (starting ~4–6 weeks after planting)


## Vocabulary


- **DLI** — daily light integral, mol/m²/day of photosynthetic light.
