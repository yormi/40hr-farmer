# Pollinate — vibrate the trellis wires

Greenhouse tomatoes. A daily wire-tap that shakes pollen loose
so flowers set. No pollination, no fruit — this **gates fruit
load** before every other lever.


## When

- **Before lunch** — driest part of the day. Damp pollen clumps
  and won't release.
- **Every weekday.** A habit is harder to forget.

Flower viability is only **1–2 days** after opening (shorter in
summer) → miss 3 days and you fall short of your fruit-load
target.


## Who

- **Delegate it.** No reason for the farm owner to do this job.


## How

- Vibrate the trellis **wires**, not the plants — tap or twang them.
- Tie tie-wraps on the wire as tapping points.
- Tape the wire in the middle, **between the two wires**.
- Tap several spots along the bed: start a quarter of the way in,
  watch where the wire shakes, move further to reach the rest.
- Do **both beds at once.**


## Experimental / bigger operations

Bumblebee hives — hands-free pollination. Worth it for:

- Screened houses.
- Houses wider than ~30 ft.
- Very early / late season with no active pollinators.
- Urban sites with no wild bees.

One hive covers ~1,000–15,000 sq ft, lasts ~7–9 weeks. For a
small mixed greenhouse, wire-tapping is usually enough.

Electric "bee" flower vibrators — not recommended.
