# Planting density

Set stems/m² from the light the crop will get at full fruit load.

Why → [`science/density.md`](../../science/density.md)


## Steps

1. Look up the region's monthly outside DLI.
2. Multiply by house transmission → inside DLI.
   - double poly: 0.6–0.65
3. Take inside DLI at full fruit load.
   - transplant: ~8–10 weeks after planting
   - crop already loaded: now
4. Divide by 7 → stems/m², clamp 2.0–4.0.
   - beefsteaks (Contessa, Kardinata): divide by 8–9
   - hot summer: go lower
5. Plant → add stems as light rises.
   - extra side shoot, every 2nd–4th plant
   - start ~4–6 weeks after planting


## Crop at full load in low light

- Hold stems at 2.0 floor.
- Prune trusses → 3–4 fruit.
  - medium types: 4–5
- Lower temperature slightly.
- Leave more fruit per truss as light rises.
  - see [`fruit-thinning.md`](../fruit-load/fruit-thinning.md)
