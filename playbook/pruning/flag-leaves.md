# Flag leaves — recover foliar area on a weak plant

Greenhouse tomatoes. Instead of snapping a sucker flush, pinch it
after its first leaf → that leaf stays on as a "flag leaf,"
adding foliar area.

A weak plant grows **small leaves**: it takes ~10 of them to make
the sugar of one 18-in leaf, and you can't fit enough. Foliar
area falls short, and sugar, irrigation, and temperature all
suffer. Flag leaves buy back the missing solar panel.


## When to use it

- Plant is throwing small leaves (common with vigor problems).
- Or you had to prune damaged leaves below the ~18-leaf target.
- Can't just prune the short leaves off a weak plant — no solar
  panel left. Cross-check `playbook/scouting/leaf-quality-check.md`.


## How — pinch, don't snap

| Situation | Pinch point |
|---|---|
| Sucker still small | after its **first** leaf |
| Late — sucker grew several leaves | after the **last** formed leaf (don't waste that growth) |

- **CRITICAL** — always pinch **before (below)** any flower
  cluster on the sucker. Let a cluster form → you add an energy
  sink bigger than the leaves produce.


## Later

- Once the plant recovers, prune flag leaves like normal leaves.
