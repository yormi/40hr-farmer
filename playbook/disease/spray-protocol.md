# Spray protocol

Greenhouse tomatoes. The **how** of spraying: timing,
conditions, re-entry. Which product per disease lives in
[`disease-control.md`](disease-control.md).


## Before

- Can it be done before 10am?
- Is the greenhouse <85°F?
- Are the leaves dry? Or are there condensation?
- Can we close the greenhouse for 24h?

A single "no" and contact the greenhouse manager to re-schedule.


## Protect yourself

Biggest danger is while mixing

- Tyvek suit
- Chemical-resistant gloves (nitrile)
- Closed shoes + socks
- Protective eyewear (safety glasses or goggles)
- Respiratory protection


## Product

- Late blight, early blight, bacterial spot: Badge X2 + Regalia together

- Powdery mildew: <85'F in the greenhouse for the next 24h?
    - Yes -> Sulfur
    - No -> Milstop


## Do

- Set reminder to remove sign in 24h
- Put sign on the door to keep-out until ____ (write time so others know)
- Mix product in tank
- Spray and aim under leaves too
- Rinse the sprayer
