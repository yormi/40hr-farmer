# Greenhouse heating reference


## Heat load

- Conduction = U × skin area × ΔT(°F)

- Infiltration = 0.018 × volume × air changes/h × ΔT(°F)

- U (BTU/h·ft²·°F): double poly 0.7, single poly 1.15, glass 1.1, twinwall polycarbonate 0.6

- 1 kW = 3,412 BTU/h. 1 GJ ≈ 40 L propane.


## Electric

- Continuous load ≤ 80% of breaker rating

- 240 V × 200 A → ≈ 38 kW usable before existing load

- Cold-climate heat pump COP ≈ 2 at −7 °C, ≈ 2.5–3 near 0 °C

- Cold-climate 3-ton mini-split: ≈ 10 kW heat, 3–4 kW draw, ≈ $8–15k CAD installed each (2026)


## Heater types

| Type | Combustion air | Exhaust | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unvented | inside | inside | ❌ ethylene → leaf curl, flower drop |
| Gravity vented | inside | chimney | ⚠️ backdraft |
| Power vented (Modine PTP/BTP) | inside | fan → pipe | ✅ standard |
| Separated combustion (Modine PTX/BTX) | outside | pipe | ✅✅ longest life, +$500–1.5k/unit |
| Condensing (Modine PTC/BTC, ≈93%) | outside | pipe + drain | propane: pays back 3–5 yr |

- P = propeller (default), B = blower (only with ducts / poly tubes, ≈ 2× price)

- Stainless heat exchanger over aluminized in humid houses
