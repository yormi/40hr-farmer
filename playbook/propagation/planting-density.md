# Planting density

Set stems/m² from the light the crop will get.

Why → [`science/density.md`](../../science/density.md)


## Steps

1. Look up the region's monthly outside DLI.
2. Multiply by house transmission → inside DLI.
   - double poly: 0.6–0.65
3. Average inside DLI over planting → ~8 weeks after.
4. Divide by 7 → stems/m², clamp 2.0–4.0.
   - beefsteaks (Contessa, Kardinata): divide by 8–9
   - hot summer: go lower
5. Plant at the low-light density → add stems as light rises.
   - extra side shoot, every 2nd–4th plant
   - start ~4–6 weeks after planting
