# Flag leaves

Pinch a sucker after a leaf instead of snapping it flush. The
leaf stays on and adds foliar area.

Why → [`science/leaf-energy.md`](../../science/leaf-energy.md)


## When to use it

- Plant is throwing small leaves.
- Or damage-pruning dropped you below the ~18-leaf target.
- Cross-check [`scouting/leaf-quality-check.md`](../scouting/leaf-quality-check.md).


## How — pinch, don't snap

| Situation | Pinch point |
|---|---|
| Sucker still small | after its **first** leaf |
| Sucker already grew several leaves | after the **last** formed leaf |


## Stop

- **Always pinch below any flower cluster on the sucker.** A
  cluster is a sink bigger than the leaves produce.
- Never on a topped plant → [`post-topping-drawdown.md`](post-topping-drawdown.md).


## Later

- Plant recovered → prune flag leaves like normal leaves.
