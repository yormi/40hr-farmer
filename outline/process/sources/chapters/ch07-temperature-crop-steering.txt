    Plants are particularly prone to becoming excessively

    The problem of excessively vegetative plants may be
particularly acute for hoophouse growers, who are lim-
ited by the weather as to how early they can plant. By the
time they can transplant, the days tend to be long, with
lots of light and heat. All these factors push the plant
to grow quickly and vegetatively, which can have a bad
effect on yield of valuable early fruit.
