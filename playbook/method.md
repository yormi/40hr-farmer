# The method

How to think about running the crop. Three layers:

1. **Mindsets** — the four modes you work in.
2. **Diagnostics** — identify the current core problem.
3. **Protocols** — the cards that guide the fix.

A mindset tells you *why* you're at the plant. Diagnostics find
*what's* wrong. Protocols say *what to do.*

Why any of it works → [`science/`](../science/README.md).


---


## Mindsets

The four modes. Each leans on the same diagnostics and protocols
below.


### Improvement project

- A deliberate level-up, usually **off-season** (few land
  in-season).
- The main one is **production infrastructure prep** — what the
  weekly loop can't change on a live crop:

| Area | Prep |
|---|---|
| **Equipment** | Trellis + wire rated for a full fruit load (~⅛ in / gauge 9); heating (propane / heaters, installed ahead); HAF fans; climate controller + backup thermostat (60°F failsafe); EC pen (~$80); irrigation system + right emitters / pressure. |
| **Soil** | Build the fertility base; correct pH so nutrients aren't locked out; flush salt build-up. |
| **Genetics** | Variety choice, including heat-tolerant / parthenocarpic; grafting / rootstock (e.g. Maxifort) for vigor and heat resilience — grafts run ~15% more vigorous. |

- Lead time is real — heaters to install, soil to amend, seed and
  rootstock to order. Planned, not last-minute.


### Planning

- Forward-looking, before the pressure hits.
- Crop plan and fruit-load targets for the season.


### Weekly check-in

- Every week, ~2 min, **one sample plant.**
- Same average plant mid-bed — not in a puddle, not by a heater,
  not in a cold draft / poly leak. Tape a trellis clip to mark it.
- Forces you to stop and read the plant; track its progress.
- Delegatable to greenhouse staff.
- Rare in farming: a **one-week feedback loop.** Change one thing
  → read the result next week → keep or revert.

Orisha shortcut: the Consultant check-in returns your fruit-load
target and yield potential.


### Troubleshoot

- A tell shows up — off-cadence or at the weekly check-in.
- Run **diagnostics** to name the core problem, then apply the
  **protocol**, one lever at a time. Re-read next week.

## Diagnostics — find the core problem

Two steps: rank which problem is live, then read the plant to
confirm it.


### Rank

Fix in this order, highest first. **Don't chase a lower problem
while a higher one is live** — a lower fix won't hold on a plant
that's failing higher up.

| Rank | Problem | Is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Low fruit load** | Not setting or carrying enough fruit. |
| 2 | **Vigor** | Plant strength — stem thin, plant overworked. |
| 3 | **Disease** | Rot, spot, mold taking fruit and leaf. |
| 4 | **Uniformity** | Bed running uneven, plant to plant. |

Equipment and soil gaps you can't fix on a live crop aren't in
this list. Log them for an off-season improvement project.


### Read the tells

Let the plant point at the bottleneck.

| Problem | Tells (read) |
|---|---|
| Low fruit load | blossom-height |
| Vigor | stem-vigor-weekly, blossom-height, leaf-quality-check, midday-leaf-cupping, slurry-ec-test |
| Disease | weekly-scout, identify-disease |
| Uniformity | *cards not yet built* |
