# Disease control — the 1-decision engine

Greenhouse tomatoes. One page for every fungal / bacterial
disease. The whole design is to leave the grower with a single
recurring decision, not five.

Design rule: a stretched grower follows a 1-decision protocol
and quietly abandons a 5-decision one. So every mid-season
judgment call is converted to a fixed rule, and we pay for that
with a little extra spray or time. A branch removed is worth
more than the spray it saves.


## Strategy

- These diseases are overwhelmingly driven by two things:
  climate (wet vs dry) and genetics (variety). Easiest levers.

- The single highest-effectiveness move is to prevent so the sprays are rarely needed.
  That's also the simplest in-season:
  fewer sprays, fewer decisions, less time, less money.

- Only then, we lean on products that keep protecting between
  weekly visits. That's what lets us spray on a weekly
  clock and walk away.


## The engine — runs under every disease

- **Keep the zone dry** Climate dries the
  leaf at least once every 90 min. This is the foundation under
  every card. Never a judgment call.

- **Prune damaged tissues**. Keep disease from settling in.

- **Scout**  *Do I see it this week?*
  No → keep drying. Yes → run that disease's block top to
  bottom.

- **One stop rule for all sprays:** no new spots for 7 straight
  days → stop spraying, keep drying.


## The cards

### Leaf mold

**If ignored**
- Yield drag from lost leaf area.
- Steady stripping labour.
- Plant survives.
- Spread to next plant: 1 week.

**Control expectation**
- Spray innefficient.
- Climate + variety are what actually clear it.
- In a wet house you pick leaves all season.

**Prevent**
- Dry + resistant variety.

**Cull**
- Affected leaves.
- At weekly scouting.

### Bacterial spot

**If ignored**
- Fruit out of the marketable pack.
- Culling + grading labour up.
- Spread to next plant: 5 days.

**Control expectation**
- Suppression, not a cure. Hold new spots down and live with
  some. Copper resistance is common.

**Prevent**
- Keep the climate dry.

**Spray**
- **Badge X2 + Regalia Mixed**, underside coverage, **7-day**.
- Pre-Harvest: 0 days.
- Re-entry: Badge X2 24 h, Regalia 4 h.

### Sclerotinia

**If ignored**
- Whole plants lost.
- Pull & start new heads.
- Soil banked 8 years.
- Spread to next plant: 2 weeks.

**Control expectation**
- The plant is lost.
- The goal is to save the bed.

**Prevent**
- Dry every 90 min.
- High pressure → apply **Contans** to soil pre-season.

**Cull**
- The **whole plant**.
- At weekly scouting.


### Late blight

**If ignored**
- Lose the house in a week.
- Spread to next plant: 1 day.

**Control expectation**
- Fast and often unwinnable once inside.
- Pulling + copper buys time
- Harder to control in unheated houses. Thrives at 55-75'F.
- Expect to lose plants, aim to save the house.

**Prevent**
- Dry every 90 min.
- Resistant variety

**Cull**
- The **whole infected plant**, out of the house.
- **On sight** — don't wait for the weekly session.
- Never snip the spotted leaf and leave the plant;
  Infection is way ahead of symptoms. The whole plant goes.

**Spray**
- **Badge X2 + Regalia Mixed**, underside coverage, **5-day**.
- Pre-Harvest: 0 days.
- Re-entry: 24 h

### Early blight

**If ignored**
- Steady yield bleed.
- Fruit rot + sunscald.
- Constant leaf labour.
- Plant survives.
- Spread to next plant: 2 weeks.

**Control expectation**
- The protocol usually holds it to the lower leaves all season.

**Prevent**
- Dry + mulch against soil splash.
- Variety with early-blight tolerance.

**Cull**
- Infected leaves.
- At weekly scouting.

**Spray**
- **Badge X2 + Regalia Mixed**, underside coverage, **7-day**.
- Pre-Harvest: 0 days.
- Re-entry: Badge X2 24 h, Cease 4 h.

### Powdery mildew

**If ignored**
- Vigour + yield sapped.
- Fruit sunscalds.
- Plant survives.
- Spread to next plant: 1 week.

**Control expectation**
- You manage it, never clear it.
- A low background haze is success, not failure.
- Accept some background mildew.

**Spray**
- **Sulfur**
- Repeat when it returns, not on a clock.
- Spray only if house stays below 85°F for 24h after the spray or you'll burn fruit and leaves
- House runs >85'F every day → MilStop instead, full both-side coverage, early AM or evening, repeat every **7 days** as long as you see symptoms.
- Never spray within ~2 weeks of an oil spray
- Pre-Harvest: 0 days (both).
- Re-entry: sulfur 24 h, MilStop 1 h.

### Botrytis

**If ignored**
- Fruit rots on the plants.
- Whole plants killed.
- Spread to next plant: 4 days.
- Stem girdle kills the whole plant.

**Control expectation**
- The protocol holds it only while the house stays dry;
  humidity brings it back.
- Expect recurrence.

**Prevent**
- Dry (the 1st tool).

**Cull**
- Fruits
- Infected + senescing leaves.
- Prune stubs. They never heal. Open door to botrytis.
- Stem → **carve it out** (you can carve ~90% of a stem and the plant lives).
- At weekly scouting.

**Spray**
- Sprays are a waste of time *on top of a dry house*
