## Diagnosis

Chief Grower doesn't sell because growers have never heard a peer say controlling the climate made their yields 5x.

Because we sold a tool to improve a greenhouse production but left the yield increase in grower's hands.

Also, growers haven't heard it from peers because early adopters are few, scattered, and were un-instrumented. A direct result of survival-driven wide
selling, which buys revenue but no proof density.

Common knowledge would recommend to focus on a single town. It doesn't work for us:
    - Early-adopters are too spread out to reach a critical mass
    - Word of mouth is slow. Once a year at conferences
    - We need more than a regional impact to fund our operations

Even at the state size, we'll still have these problems.

The crux to Orisha's adoption is to get one community flooded by
stories of growers like them that drastically increase their greenhouse
yields.

On the other hand, we're a very small team for changing how the world grows in greenhouses on small farms.


## Guiding policy

Since we have limited capacity, we'll focus aggressively.

First, we'll focus only on growing tomatoes. We'll sell the tomato yield outcome, not the tool.
We'll ensure every existing and new customer has a success story with visible weekly yields numbers.

Second, we'll leverage those stories by flooding only ONE community :
    - One community that is big enough to have enough early-adopters
    - One community that is big enough to have thousands of potential customers
    - One community in which news move fast
    - One community that is loud enough to reach people no matter the season.

GFM is that community. It act as a village where we can both flood
with wins and reach the volume of farmers we need to fund our operation.

Third, the flood runs on two cadences:

- **Base load, weekly.** Shows many farms succeeds.
    - Yield feedback loop — grower reports in weekly check-in
    - Tracker board — updates the week the numbers land
    - Instagram — weekly yields report + testimonials

- **Deep layer, monthly.** Make wins credible and human.
    - Magazine — the season's stories, told fully
    - Podcast — one grower's arc, in their voice

Sales, content, and product decisions are judged by one test: does this add a
visible successful farm to the village flood.

If it doesn't, sell to them, support them, never spend a marketing or product hour on them.
We just don't build for their use case.
