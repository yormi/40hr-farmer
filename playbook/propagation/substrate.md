# Propagation substrate

One bag, every stage, 2 fish drenches. Grafted and non-grafted alike.

Dates → [`seedling-transplant-calendar.md`](seedling-transplant-calendar.md)


## Rule

- Buy [**Vermont Compost Fort Vee**](https://www.7springsfarm.com/products/vermont-compost-fort-vee-potting-mix-45-qt). Use straight from the bag.
- 128-cell 1020 trays: seed rootstock, scion, non-grafted.
- Pot up to 4 in: same mix.
- Add nothing to the mix.
- Fish drench 2×, grafted + non-grafted together:
  - Transplant − 14 days
  - Transplant − 7 days
  - [Alaska Fish Fertilizer 5-1-1](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Alaska-128-oz-1-gal-Organic-Gardening-Liquid-Fish-Emulsion-Plant-Food-Fertilizer-Concentrate-5-1-1-100099249/100211859), OMRI
  - 2 tbsp/gal
  - Water to slight runoff, ~3.5 fl oz per 4 in pot
  - 1 gal jug ≈ 2,400 pots (2 drenches each)
  - Nothing in the 128-cell stage

## Fixed rules

- Surface dry between waterings. Water by weight, not schedule.
- Root zone 20–22 °C. Cold roots → no nutrient release.
- Never home or unknown compost/manure → We want predictability. We can't go blindly


## Yield at stake

Estimates, not measured. Transplant N shifts early yield; season total mostly recovers.

| Scenario | Early yield (clusters 1 to 3) |
|---|---|
| Underfed, mild (pale by the last week) | −10 to −20% |
| Underfed, severe (purple and stalled for weeks) | −20 to −40% |
| Overfed, mild (soft, dark) | −5 to −15% |
| Overfed, severe (burnt roots, bull plants) | −15 to −30% |



## Costly deficiencies

| Nutrient | Signs | Likely cause here | Cost | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **N** | Older leaves pale, then yellow. Thin, hard stems | Front-load used up in weeks 7 to 9 of the grafted pot hold | Fewer flowers on clusters 1 to 3, −10 to −40% early yield | Fish drench |
| **P** | Purple leaf undersides and stems, dark dull foliage, stalled growth | **Cold root zone** (under ~16 °C) blocking uptake. Rarely a real shortage | Weaker roots and fewer flowers on the first clusters. Stalls add days | Warm the roots to 20–22 °C. Adding P won't help while roots are cold |
| **Fe** (+ Mn) | Young leaves yellow between the veins, veins stay green | Mix pH over ~7. Compost mixes run higher than peat | Slower head growth, which can delay the pinch and transplant | Test pH first. If deficiency is confirmed, use an OMRI iron product |

- Rare in propagation → skip: K, Mg, Ca, B.
- Purple in March → cold roots, not missing P. Warm, don't feed.
