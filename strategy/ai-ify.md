# AI-ify — ideas

# 1. Consulting

## Storyline and thinking
- Pressure-test the "so what" of a storyline
- Use AI to find holes in the logic before growers do (partnership wizard, funnel)
- Structuring problem solving

## Team facilitation
- Find questions to guide the team to go deeper

## Meetings
- Meeting prep
- Meeting follow-up: draft email with a Rumelt strategy summary (diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions)

# 2. Content (Instagram)

## Ideation
- Ideation from real audience language: feed comments, DMs, reviews and call transcripts to find hooks and post ideas built on real questions and objections
  - [JEV] Ideation from user posts and transcripts: Jev sorts and tags comments, DMs and call transcripts by theme/objection to surface post ideas

## Writing
- Hooks and captions: generate 10–20 hook variants per idea, tighten captions to brand voice, add CTAs
  - [JEV] Laugh Track: pre-test hooks and Reel scripts line by line on ~200 simulated grower listeners
- Reel scripts and shot lists: 30-second script (hook, beats, on-screen text) plus the farm shots to film
- Carousel outlines: hook slide, one idea per slide, CTA slide (design in Canva)

## Production
- Repurposing: turn one long piece (podcast, webinar, blog, grower call) into carousels, Reel scripts, quotes and Stories
  - [JEV] Finding clips: Jev scores each transcript segment of a long video/call to pick the best clip moments
- Visuals: image generation for backgrounds and mockups; AI editing for captions, silence cuts, B-roll (CapCut, Descript, Opus Clip)

## Performance
- Performance review: paste post insights, ask what separates winners from flops, decide what to test next
  - [JEV] Report Card: grade drafts on hook, clarity and CTA
  - Define success first: pick one primary goal per post (reach, saves/shares, profile visits, DM keyword triggers, funnel leads)
  - Set a baseline from the last ~20 posts and judge each post against it, not in absolute numbers
  - Track a few leading metrics: hook hold rate (3-sec views), saves + shares per reach, DMs/link clicks per reach
  - Tie it to the funnel: a winner is a post that produces qualified DM conversations or leads, not just likes
  - Review on a fixed cadence (e.g. every 2 weeks) and change one variable per test
