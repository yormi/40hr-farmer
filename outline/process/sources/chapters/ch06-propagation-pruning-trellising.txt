    Pruning is one of the techniques that allows growers
to crowd as many plants as possible into a finite area, by
limiting each plant to the minimum amount of space it
needs. Pruning not only conserves space by preventing
vines from branching out, but it also makes a plant more
open and permits light and air to flow through the can-
opy. This results in a minimal amount of yield reduction
due to shading and disease. Fruit on pruned plants sizes
up and ripens more quickly than on unpruned plants.
And all crops that are kept pruned are easier to harvest,
because you don’t have to hunt through as much foliage
to locate the fruit.
