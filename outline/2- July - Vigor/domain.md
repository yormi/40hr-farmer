# Domain — Plant Vigor (Month 2)


## Model


### Energy balance


- photosynthesis → energy pool   (production; driven by light, CO2, warmth, water)

- energy pool → **vegetative sink** + **generative sink**   (the pool splits between two sinks)

- **vegetative sink** (leaf + stem) ↔ **generative sink** (fruit + flower)   (trade-off: energy to one is energy denied the other)

- vigor = production − consumption   (the plant's net energy level, not which sink wins)

- stress → shifts the split toward **generative**; comfort → shifts it toward **vegetative**   (steering moves the split, not the pool size)


### Reading the head


- the **head** shows the current split between the two sinks

- thick stem + growth above the cluster → reads **vegetative**

- thin stem + cluster near the tip → reads **generative**

- stem caliper at the head → one read of which sink leads

- head signals read together: stem caliper, truss angle, leaf size and shape, tip-to-open-flower distance, flower color   (one signal alone is noisy)


### Caliper baseline by fruit size


- fruit-size class → balanced caliper baseline   (bigger fruit drives a fatter stem to feed the larger sink)

- **fruit-size class** 1—1 **caliper baseline**   (each class carries its own baseline)

- caliper trend vs that baseline → vigor direction; absolute caliper alone does NOT   (thinning + slowing → generative; thickening + accelerating → vegetative)

- pink-beef baseline < red-beef baseline at equal fruit size   (heirloom genetics, not weak vigor)


### Caliper bands


| Class | Balanced stem Ø | Felt object | Edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red beef | ~12–14 mm | AA battery (~14 mm) | ~19 mm (0.75 in) → too vegetative |
| Pink beef | ~11–13 mm | Sharpie barrel | thinner than red beef |
| Cocktail | ~10–11 mm | AAA battery | — |
| Cherry / grape | ~7–9 mm | wooden pencil | at or under a AAA at full vigor |

Caliper is the stem Ø just below the youngest truss with an open flower (equivalently, 6 in / 15 cm below the tip).


## Vocabulary


**head** — the top 4–8 in (10–20 cm) of new growth at the stem tip; the window where vigor and the vegetative/generative split are read.
