# Slurry EC test — root-zone salts

Greenhouse tomatoes. A 1:1 (by weight) soil-water slurry read
with the EZ Pro pen. Tells you whether root-zone salts are in
band before deciding to fertilize, hold, or flush.

1:1 **by weight, not volume**: soil bulk density drifts with
moisture, so weight is what stays comparable week to week.


## Before the batch

- Check water EC. >~0.15 mS/cm → subtract from readings;
  tap (~0.10) → ignore.


## Per round — batch all beds at once

1. Each bed: 5 cores at root-zone depth (~10 cm) → combine in
   its cup, skip obvious stones / roots.
2. Scoop soil into the cup → note its weight.
3. Add the same weight of water.
4. Stir each ~60 s.
5. One timer → whole batch stands 1 min.
6. Read each → rinse pen between cups → record EC_slurry.


## Reading the pen

- Hold the probe in the **mid-liquid zone**. Don't put the
  probe in the sludge at the bottom — particles foul the
  electrodes and make the number jump.
- Swirl, wait for the number to settle, record.


## Accept band — 1:1 (w/w), tomato

Pick your soil's column. EC_slurry in mS/cm. No multiplier —
read the pen straight against the band.

| State | Sand | Loam | Clay | Do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| low | < 0.5 | < 1.0 | < 1.3 | feed due |
| in band | 0.5–1.0 | 1.0–2.0 | 1.3–2.7 | normal schedule |
| high | 1.0–1.25 | 2.0–2.5 | 2.7–3.3 | postpone next feed |
| salt buildup | > 1.25 | > 2.5 | > 3.3 | flush, recheck |

Low end leans vegetative / vigor; high end leans generative.

Same greenhouse-tomato saturated-media target across all three
columns; they differ only because a 1:1 (w/w) slurry dilutes
each texture differently (sand most, clay least). Lock your
column with one paired lab SME test, or by reading against
same-day runoff EC, and shift to match.
