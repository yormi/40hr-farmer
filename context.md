# Language

Canonical vocabulary for the 40hr Farmer program (copy, emails, video
outlines). Greenhouse-tomato terms whose meaning is fixed for this project.

**Plant vigor**:
How strongly and steadily a plant is growing, read off visible signals:
stem caliper one foot below the top, leaves per **leader**, fruit set per
cluster, and blossom drop. The thing Month 2 of the program teaches the
farmer to control. Managed with several levers, **vegetative vs generative
steering** being one.
_Avoid_: plant health (broader), plant strength; do not equate with
vegetative vs generative balance (that is one lever, not vigor itself).

**Vegetative vs generative steering**:
Pushing a plant toward leaf and stem growth (vegetative) versus toward
flowering and fruit (generative). One of the levers a farmer uses to manage
**plant vigor**, alongside climate, irrigation, pruning, feeding, and fruit
load.

**Leader**:
A single trained growing stem. Standard practice is two leaders per root
system (one plant grows two leaders). Density and pruning are counted in
leaders, not plants.

**Leaders per square foot**:
The planting-density unit the program uses (research and calculators are
built on it). Optimal is 0.27 leaders/sq.ft for beef tomatoes. Counted via
**leaders**, not plant count.
_Avoid_: plant density, plants per bed

**AA-battery stem**:
The visible benchmark for a healthy plant: the stem one foot below the
growing top is as thick as a AA battery. A thinner, spindly stem signals
the plant is consuming more energy than it produces.
