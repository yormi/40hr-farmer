# Fit call

45 min · video · share [Fit Call deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1_Hi7YRd3PJNBny4r1THe3UOuS19BEh1PS7O2f6Ktn24/edit) slides 2 to 8.

Farmer leaves with: yield goal · strategy · honest yes or no.


## 1. Open · slide 2 - 30 sec

- Show agenda
  - farm 15 · goal + strategy 15 · decision 10 · questions 5
- State what they leave with


## 2. Your farm today · slide 3 · 5 min

- What do you want for your farm next year?
- What don't you want again next year?

- Ask Greenhouse
  - How many greehouses ?
  - how many tomato beds ?
  - bed size ?
  - heater, 2 wires, irrigation
- Ask capacity
  - How many hours per week ?
  - Who does the work
- Last season
  - Weeks of tomato sales
  - Harvest in $/week



## 3. Strategy · slide 4 - 10 min

- Ask market
  - Where they sell
  - Price
  - sell earlier, more, later?
  - If you could harvest for $XXX,XXX, could you sell it ?
    - Go in $/week + period if needed

- What about your experience with
  - Fruit setting
  - Disease


## 4. Goal for 2027 · slide 5 - 5min

- Fill table: tomatoes harvested per week, lb
  - today
  - 2027 goal

- Bed available ?
- Labor available ?


## 5. What could hold you back · slide 6 - 10min

- If we work together, what's most likely to hold you back?
- What else competes for cash and hours
  - implement, new crop, wash station, more from tunnel


## 6. Best move? · slide 7 - 10min

- What other project planned for next year ?
    - What goal do you want to reach with that
    - How to measure the impact ?
    - Fits the goal stated above ?

- Say : Are tomatoes their best move?


## 7. Decision · slide 8 · 5 min

- Check qualifiers: labor, method, market, infrastructure
- Yes → shake hands → planning starts November 1
- No → they keep goal + strategy
