# Spray protocol

The how of spraying. Product per disease lives in
[`disease-control.md`](disease-control.md).

Why → [`science/disease.md`](../../science/disease.md)


## Gate — all four must pass

- Can it be done before 10am?
- Is the house below 85 °F?
- Are the leaves dry, no condensation?
- Can the house be closed for 24 h?

Any "no" → contact the greenhouse manager to reschedule.


## Protect yourself

Biggest exposure is while mixing.

- Tyvek suit
- Chemical-resistant nitrile gloves
- Closed shoes + socks
- Safety glasses or goggles
- Respiratory protection


## Product

| Disease | Product |
|---|---|
| Late blight, early blight, bacterial spot | Badge X2 + Regalia |
| Powdery mildew, house below 85 °F for 24 h | Sulfur |
| Powdery mildew, house above 85 °F | MilStop |


## Do

1. Set a reminder to remove the sign in 24 h.
2. Put the sign on the door with the keep-out time written on it.
3. Mix product in tank.
4. Spray. Aim under leaves too.
5. Rinse the sprayer.
