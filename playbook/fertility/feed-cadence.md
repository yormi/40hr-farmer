# Feed cadence

How much nitrogen, how often, and how to correct off an EC read.

Why → [`science/water.md`](../../science/water.md)

Interim. Micronutrients (Zn / Mn / Cu / Fe) not covered.


## Cadence

- **Once every 2 to 3 weeks.** Frequent small doses.
- No front-loading.


## Read before feeding

Run [`scouting/slurry-ec-test.md`](../scouting/slurry-ec-test.md)
for method and per-soil bands.

| EC read | Do |
|---|---|
| In band | feed on normal cadence |
| Low | rule out leaching first, then feed more |
| High | hold until it falls back into band |


## Correcting off-band

- **Low** — check for over-long irrigation leaching before
  increasing fertility.
- **High** — wait. Give less at a time. You can't front-load out
  of it.


## Scale with the crop

- More fruit load and growth → the feed program scales up.
- Organic fertility lags. Don't chase it fast.


## Stop

- An EC pen tells you **how much**, never **what's missing**.
  That needs a soil test.
