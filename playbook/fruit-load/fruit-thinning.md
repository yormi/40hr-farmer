# Fruit thinning

Prune clusters to a fixed fruit count. You set the load, not the
plant.

Why → [`science/fruit.md`](../../science/fruit.md)


## Target — summer

- **No more than 3 fruit per cluster, all clusters.**
- Ceiling while load is low: **4**.
- Consistency across the bed matters more than the exact number.


## When

- Start thinning to 3 **~8 weeks before** heat-wave season.
- Prune **weekly**.


## How

1. Prune **as early as possible**, before fruit reaches full
   size.
2. On big clusters, take the **smaller** fruit.
3. Cut **tiny laggards** far behind their clustermates.
4. Prune **evenly across clusters**.


## Stop

- **Don't prune full-size fruit.** The sugar is already spent —
  you lose the sale and recover nothing.
