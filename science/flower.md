# Flower — reading plant energy off the bloom


## Model


### What a flower reports


- flower tissue is built over the **~2 weeks before** it opens → a flower reports the plant's energy state a fortnight ago   (same lag as pollen, [`fruit.md`](fruit.md))

- flower size + color → the energy pool, 1 to 2 weeks **before** caliper moves   (the fastest cheap vigor read; caliper reference in [`vigor.md`](vigor.md))

- the read is **relative and same-week**: this week's fresh flowers vs last week's, and vs the rest of the bed   (bed-wide drift is the signal, one flower is noise)

- read **freshly opened flowers only** (day 1–2), in natural light   (every flower fades paler with age; that is aging, not a signal)


### Color


| Read | Anchor | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Strong | dandelion in full sun, egg yolk — orange-leaning, saturated | energy pool full |
| Weak | butter, straw; whitish petal tips | sugar-short 2 weeks ago |


### Size


| Read | Beefsteak anchor | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Strong | beer-bottle cap or wider (26–28 mm) | petals recurve past the sepals |
| Boundary | quarter (24 mm, US or CA) | watch, don't act |
| Weak | dime (18 mm, US or CA) | pinched star, petals ≈ sepal length |

- **variety class → its own size baseline**   (cherry and cocktail flowers run small by design; compare within variety, never across)

- **anther cone**, same glance: fat and closed → strong; slack or split → stressed


### Strong truss at set


- strong cluster = big saturated flowers + **thick, short, kinked truss stem** that arcs downward

- thin, long, straight truss pointing up → starved; will carry small fruit or abort

- the truss-stem signals travel together → one read covers them: **candy cane or car antenna?** A strong truss arcs its flowers at the ground before any fruit weight exists; a weak one points up or level. Readable from the alley at walking pace.

- tie-break when the curve is ambiguous: thickness at the base — **pencil** strong, **matchstick** weak

- judge at or just after flowering   (fruit weight later bends every truss down and fakes the read)

- set completeness and fruitlet uniformity → [`fruit.md`](fruit.md) (pollination and set)


## Invariants


- Flower quality is a trailing report, not a live state: it shows conditions from two weeks ago, so a fix applied today shows in flowers in two weeks.


## Boundaries


- Covers reading the energy pool off flower appearance. Pollen viability, heat damage, and set → [`fruit.md`](fruit.md). Caliper and head reads → [`vigor.md`](vigor.md). What to do about a weak read → playbook.
