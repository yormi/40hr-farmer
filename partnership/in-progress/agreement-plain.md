# Partnership Agreement

## Our deal

You grow and sell the tomatoes. We bring the gear, the plan, and weekly coaching for one greenhouse. Together, we push our yields up.


## We bring

- Climate control and irrigation automation gear for one greenhouse.
- Step by step instructions, built with you from choosing varieties to crop out.
- Every week, we read your Monday numbers and tell you what to adjust.
- Technical help by phone, Monday to Friday, 9 am to 4 pm ET.
- Antoine answers growing questions by email within the week, and reaches out when your numbers look off.


## You bring

- 3 to 4 hours per bed per week, tending the beds.
- A 2 minute check-in every Monday.
- An email to us as soon as something is off.
- Your own propane, heating, dehumidification, and labor costs.


## Tell us early

This is how we get our yields up. Write us as soon as:

- a step doesn't work on your farm or in your week.
- you see something off in the greenhouse.

No issue is too small. A short email today can save us weeks of lost yield.


## Ready by January 31, 2027

- Automation gear installed.
- 2 wire lower and lean trellis.
- A heater that keeps it above 65°F on spring nights.
- Drip irrigation with constant pressure that won't freeze.
- Money set aside for propane, for heat and dehumidification.
- Indeterminate slicer, heirloom, or cherry varieties, to transplant between February 1 and April 30, 2027.


## Our season

- **January 31:** farm ready.
- **February 1 to April 30:** transplant.
- **Every Monday:** 2 minute check-in. We read it and send you what to adjust.
- **Anytime:** something off or not practical? Email us.
- **End of season:** you compare your tomatoes to last year.


## What it costs

$350 USD a month, however much more we harvest. Every extra dollar of tomato sales is yours.


## Our promise

If the extra tomatoes don't cover what you paid us, we pay you back every dollar of fees for the season.

- You judge. We trust you to be fair with us.
- You need to fill in the Monday check-in from transplant to crop out to qualify. That's how we catch problems before they cost us yields.
- Propane and labor are yours, and are not refunded.


## Showing what small farms can do

Our season can show other farmers what a small greenhouse can grow. Help us tell that story: your numbers, photos of the beds, a few words from you.


## Leaving

- Either of us can end the partnership anytime. An email is enough.
- When we part ways, you ship the gear back at your cost.
- Leaving mid-season? The refund promise still holds.
- The most Orisha can ever owe you is what you paid us.
