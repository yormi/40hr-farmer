# Andrew's outline skeletons (from Brainstorm tab, Jun 2026)

Verbatim-ish capture of the "Outline Skeletons" section Andrew added to
the July - Vigor Doc. Input for Stage 2 clustering, not a locked cut.

## Video 1 — Heat steals vigor (above what temperature?)

1. Enter the hot season with as much vigor as possible; heat will weaken it.
2. Balancing vigor matters too: enough but unbalanced still steals yield.
3. The 4 consequences of vigor going wrong (stakes of failing).
4. How to bank vigor before heat: grafting, good fertility with fertigation
   and/or sidedressing, timely pruning of leaves and suckers, down to
   3 fruits 8 weeks before hot weather.
   - Open gap: what counts as "hot weather" so different climates can customize?

## Video 2 — Read the plant, balance it

1. Signs a plant is too vegetative, generative, or balanced (reading vigor).
2. Anything that stresses the plant → more generative; anything that makes
   it happy → more vegetative.
3. Temperature is uncontrollable in summer; later videos cover the levers
   you can control.
4. Grape/cherry varieties run skinnier; most beefsteaks ~0.5 in (AA battery)
   when balanced.

## Video 3 — Climate steering

Good spot to explain greenhouse set points; shows how impossible manual
management would be.

1. Temperature average, sensor placement.
2. Day/night differential matters for development; lower nights can offset
   hot days, only to a point.
   - Open gap: lowest recommended night temperature? 60F?
3. Flat profile = vegetative; wide gap = fruit.
4. More clusters = run cooler; show how Orisha does it.
5. Cloudy days drop toward 63F.
6. Pre-night treatment: rapid end-of-day drop = more generative.
   - Open gap: Orisha does this automatically if plants too vegetative?
7. No pre-night treatment in cloudy weather, not enough energy.
