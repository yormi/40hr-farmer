Outlines for 40hr Farmer program videos. 3 min ideal, 5 min max.

- Drafts: `outline/in-progress/<topic>.md`

## Non-negotiables for outlines

- **Story first.** Open with a real, specific scene (named farmer when possible; Guillaume's own field anecdote when not). Lecture is the failure mode.
- **Felt stake.** Every number paired with what it means in lived terms. See `brand/docs/brand/voice-and-values.md` and the "numbers need felt translation" rule.
- **Never extrapolate.** Only what the farmer actually said or reported (no inferred feelings or lifestyle), even when thesis-consistent.
- **One action per video.** Conclude with one concrete thing the viewer does this week, not a list.
- **Brand voice.** Quiet farm journal, not agronomy lecture. See `brand/docs/brand/voice-and-values.md`.
- **No dashes** in copy output. Use commas, periods, colons, or rewrite.
