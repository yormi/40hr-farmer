# Slurry EC test

1:1 soil-water slurry, read with an EC pen. Tells you to feed,
hold, or flush.

Why → [`science/water.md`](../../science/water.md)


## Before the batch

- Check source-water EC. Above ~0.15 mS/cm → subtract from every
  reading. Tap (~0.10) → ignore.


## Per round — batch all beds

1. Each bed: 5 cores at ~10 cm → combine in its cup. Skip stones
   and roots.
2. Scoop soil into the cup → note its **weight**.
3. Add the **same weight** of water.
4. Stir each ~60 s.
5. One timer → whole batch stands 1 min.
6. Read each → rinse pen between cups → record.


## Reading the pen

- Probe in the **mid-liquid zone**, never the sludge.
- Swirl, wait for the number to settle, record.


## Accept band — 1:1 (w/w), mS/cm

| State | Sand | Loam | Clay | Do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| low | < 0.5 | < 1.0 | < 1.3 | feed due |
| in band | 0.5–1.0 | 1.0–2.0 | 1.3–2.7 | normal schedule |
| high | 1.0–1.25 | 2.0–2.5 | 2.7–3.3 | postpone next feed |
| salt buildup | > 1.25 | > 2.5 | > 3.3 | flush, recheck |

No multiplier. Read the pen straight against the band.
