# Month 2+ videos: cast by who knows the beat, not a fixed format

2026-06-01. A video is carried by one OR two people, and each beat is led
by whoever has the most real authority on that point (Andrew Mefferd for
book/breadth agronomy, Antoine for lean/practitioner moves). We first set
"every video is a two-person dialogue," then relaxed it twice: a video can
be solo when one expert owns every beat, and Guillaume need not be on
camera (any pairing or solo allowed). Reason: the person talking should
always be the one who knows the beat cold; format should never force a
second speaker. Template encodes this in `outline/template.md` (Speaker
convention); the Month 2 pipeline is in
`outline/in-progress/month2-vigor/process.md`.
