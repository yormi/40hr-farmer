                                                                     If you have a really low greenhouse, you can use an alter-
                                                                     native pruning strategy to keep four-stemmed eggplant
                                                                     really low. Start by growing each plant with four stems.
                                                                     This will naturally keep the plants lower by dividing the
                                                                     energy from the roots among all four heads.

                                                                     top the plants, snapping or cutting the growing points off
